Most students prepare for the application. Few prepare for the interview. 🎤

I noticed that many students don’t know about interviews like the UK CAS interview until it’s almost too late. 
So I built Jiggasha (জিজ্ঞাসা), which means “question” in Bangla.
It’s a free, beginner-friendly guide to study-abroad interviews for 🇬🇧 the UK, 🇺🇸 the USA and 🇦🇺 Australia.

Inside:
✈️ Step-by-step journeys, from offer to decision
🎬 A “how it goes” walkthrough of each interview
🎤 25+ practice questions per country, with a timer and hints
🧱 A simple way to build answers: point, reason, example
🚩 Common reasons for refusal
📄 Document and interview-day checklists
